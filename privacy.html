Privacy policy

Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver)

This policy describes Best Freelance Platforms as published today. It will be updated before any analytics, forms, or mail tooling go live.

What this draft collects

Nothing from visitors beyond hosting. These static pages do not set cookies, do not load analytics scripts, and do not include contact forms or comment systems.

Hosting

This site runs on Vercel. Vercel and network providers may process standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps) as part of delivering the site. We do not control every intermediary log.

Future tools

If we add Search Console verification, privacy-respecting analytics, or a newsletter, we will update this page before those tools are enabled and describe what is collected. We will not claim “we don’t use cookies” while running cookie-based tools.

Affiliate and outbound links

Outbound links to freelance platforms and other sites are governed by those sites’ own privacy policies. Compensated links, when approved, may be tracked by the affiliate network.

About this publication

Updated Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver)

Best Freelance Platforms is an independent research site for people deciding how to hire or get paid online. We focus on total cost, escrow and milestone mechanics, dispute limits, and situations where marketplaces are a poor fit.

Editorial method

  • Fee and policy claims cite an official URL and a “last checked” date.
  • Unverified numbers are omitted, never invented.
  • Hands-on observations (if added later) are labeled separately from source research.
  • We do not fabricate star ratings, traffic claims, or “tested by us” badges.

Sister site

Best Freelance Websites covers hiring discovery by task (for example, where to hire a logo designer). This site covers platform decision research. We cross-link when the reader needs the other next step.

Ownership and conflicts

This site and Best Freelance Websites are operated under the same management. Management also builds Trustless Network, described officially as XRPL escrow for freelancers and Web3 work. Product mentions include a clear ownership disclosure and stay within plain, verified facts.

Corrections

If a fee or policy changes, we update the page and refresh the last-checked date. A project contact email for corrections will be listed here once it is enabled.

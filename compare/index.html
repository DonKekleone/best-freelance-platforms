FAQ

Do you rank platforms?

No. Comparisons explain fee math, protection rules, and fitting use-cases from official sources. We do not publish overall rankings or star ratings.

Why do some Upwork pages still say 7.99%?

Upwork’s public client pricing page (checked Nov 5, 2026) leads with a 5% Basic service fee and 10% Business Plus, with 3%/8% bank discounts for eligible U.S. checking-account payers. Some FAQ/schema blocks on the same page still mention 7.99%. New comparisons use the hero/schema 5%/10% figures and flag the leftover 7.99% text as a conflict to re-check at checkout.

99designs fees explained

Last checked: Nov 7, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official 99designs Help Center + designer page

Short version. On a 1-to-1 Project, the client pays a fixed 5% platform fee. The designer pays a separate platform fee by level: 15% Entry, 10% Mid, 5% Top. With a new client, the designer also pays a client introduction fee of 20% of the first $500 paid, capped at $100, unless a waiver applies.

What the fees cover

  • 99designs says the platform fee covers secure payment holding, dispute resolution, anti-fraud work, payout processing, educational resources and platform development.
  • The introduction fee is pitched as the cost of bringing clients to designers (promotion, search, paid ads and matching).
  • Help recommends building both designer fees into your quoted price using the "Create a quote" calculator, which shows what you will earn after fees.

$500 first project example (illustrative)

Assume an Entry Level designer, a brand-new client found on 99designs (no waiver), a single $500 quote, and that both designer fees apply to the quoted price. Ignore FX, payout method costs and tax. Check the quote calculator for the real number.

  • Client platform fee: 5% of $500 = $25.
  • Designer platform fee at Entry Level: 15% of $500 = $75.
  • Client introduction fee: 20% of $500 = $100 (this hits the $100 cap, so later invoices with this client carry no introduction fee).
  • Designer keeps roughly $325 on this first project; on the next $500 with the same client, roughly $425 at Entry Level, or $475 at Top Level.

FAQ

What does a 99designs client pay on a 1-to-1 Project?

The client pays a fixed 5% platform fee on each 1-to-1 Project. It appears as its own line item on the designer's quote.

What platform fee does a 99designs designer pay?

Designers pay a platform fee by level: 15% at Entry Level, 10% at Mid Level and 5% at Top Level.

What is the 99designs client introduction fee?

When a designer starts working with a new client, 99designs deducts 20% from the first $500 of paid invoices with that client, capped at $100 total. It is waived if the designer won or was a finalist in a contest with that client, or invited a client who had never used 99designs through the email invitation feature.

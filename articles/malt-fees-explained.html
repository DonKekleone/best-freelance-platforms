Fees · Malt

Malt fees explained

Last checked: Nov 6, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official Malt Help (two articles) · Not a quote

Short version. Malt bills both sides. Clients pick Starter / Advanced / Enterprise and pay a plan % on the freelancer’s invoice (excl. tax), with regional tables that differ from the headline 10%/15%. Freelancers pay a service fee that Help most recently describes as 5% excl. tax (higher on some FR/ES/BE projects for the first six months). Always use the live quote / plan screen.
Official conflict (flagged). Freelancer Help (updated Oct 30, 2026) says standard freelance fees are 5% excl. tax, with certain France/Spain/Belgium projects at 10% → 5% after six months. Client-fees Help (updated June 17, 2026) instead lists freelancer commission as 10% first mission, 5% after six months, and 2% when the freelancer invites the client. We do not pick a winner; budget from the on-screen fee Malt shows on the quote.

How to budget without inventing a single %

  • Confirm your country’s client plan % (regional tables beat the headline 10%/15%).
  • On the freelancer side, treat the quote creation screen as the live number; Help says Malt shows net payout there.
  • VAT / tax on Malt’s own service fees can change the effective cost for micro-entrepreneurs (French Help discusses TTC uplift); that is tax treatment, not an extra marketplace take-rate.

FAQ

What do Malt clients pay?

Clients choose Starter, Advanced, or Enterprise. Help lists headline Starter 10% and Advanced 15% service fees on the freelancer invoice amount excl. tax, with Enterprise custom. Regional tables differ (for example Belgium Starter 5% / Advanced 9%; Middle East Starter 15% / Advanced 20%). Clients receive a separate Malt service-fee invoice beside the freelancer invoice.

What do Malt freelancers pay?

The freelancer Help article (updated Oct 30, 2026) states freelance service fees are 5% excl. tax, with certain France/Spain/Belgium projects starting at 10% excl. tax then dropping to 5% after six months. A separate client-fees Help article lists freelancer commission as 10% first mission, 5% after six months, and 2% when the freelancer invites the client. Treat the quote screen as authoritative until Malt reconciles the docs.

Are Malt fees shown before you commit?

Yes for freelancers: Help says the exact freelancer service fee and net payout appear on the quote creation page. Clients should confirm the live plan % for their country on Malt pricing or with their account manager.

Fiverr vs Contra: buyer fees and commission models

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Different product shapes

Short version. Fiverr is a Gig marketplace with percentage buyer fees and a large seller commission. Contra markets 0% Independent commission with flat client platform fees ($15 / $29 on Free) plus processor fees — better when you already have (or can find) an Independent on Contra.
Fee shape (sourced; processing fees extra on Contra)
FiverrContra
Client / buyer fee5.5% + $3.50 under $200Free plan: $15 (<$500) or $29 ($500+); $0 if Independent is on Pro
Freelancer take-rate~20% (seller keeps ~80%)0% Independent commission (Contra claim)
Discovery modelOpen Gig catalogNetwork / contracts oriented; not a Fiverr-scale Gig grid

Worked $400 and $2,000 (platform fee lens)

  • $400: Fiverr buyer fee 5.5%=$22 (no under-$200 add-on). Contra Free=$15.
  • $2,000: Fiverr buyer $110; seller keeps ~$1,600 before withdrawal. Contra Free client $29; Independent keeps $2,000 before processor/payout fees.

Deep dives: Fiverr fees, Contra commission-free.

FAQ

Is Contra cheaper on small jobs?

Not always. Contra’s $15 flat fee on a $80 job is a large effective %. Fiverr’s 5.5%+$3.50 can be lower or higher depending on size — run both checkouts.

Does commission-free mean free?

No. Contra Free clients still pay $15 or $29, and processors charge separately.

Contra fees and the commission-free model

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Primary source: Contra Help — Fees overview (updated Aug 6, 2026)

Translate the marketing. “Commission-free” on Contra means independents are not charged a % take-rate on earnings the way Fiverr (~20%) or many marketplace seller fees work. It does not mean every payment on the platform has $0 fees. Clients, processors, and payout rails still charge money.

What freelancers (Independents) pay

  • 0% commission on project earnings per Contra’s commission-free positioning and pricing pages.
  • Payout processor fees (Stripe, Airwallex, PayPal, Payoneer, Coinbase, etc.) are passed to the Independent and shown at withdrawal — amounts vary by method, country, and currency. Contra’s Fees overview documents many local-bank and FX rates; confirm the live table before you cash out.
  • Crypto payouts: Fees overview lists 2% on Free plans and 1% on Pro.
  • Contra Pro (optional): help center lists $29/month or $199/year (~$17/month billed annually). Pro waives platform fees, digital-product fees, and faster-payout fees, and cuts crypto payout fees 50% per the Pro article.

What clients pay (platform fees)

Contra’s Fees overview (checked Nov 5, 2026) lists platform fees capped at $29/transaction:

Contra platform fees by payment size
Payment sizeFree planPro (Independent on Pro)
$1–$499$15$0
$500+$29$0

Separately, payment processing fees apply (charged by processors, not listed as Contra commission). Fees overview examples include domestic card ~2.9% + 30¢, ACH 0.8% capped at $5 (eligibility required), and other methods. Always use the checkout breakdown.

Worked examples (illustrative)

Assume Free-plan Independent, one payment, ignore tax. Processing fee omitted from platform-fee column.

  • $400 project: client platform fee $15; Independent keeps $400 before payout-rail fees.
  • $1,000 project: client platform fee $29; Independent keeps $1,000 before payout-rail fees.
  • Same $1,000 with Independent on Pro: client platform fee $0; Independent still pays Pro subscription + any payout processor/FX fees.

Compare that shape to Fiverr (client 5.5% + under-$200 $3.50; seller ~20%) or Upwork (client up to 7.99% Basic; freelancer 0–15%) in our fee comparison and Upwork vs Fiverr guides.

When Contra’s model fits

  • Fits: Direct relationships where the freelancer wants 0% take-rate and the client will accept a flat platform fee + processor fee; creatives already using Contra’s workspace/contracts.
  • Less ideal: Buyers who need a giant open marketplace of proposals; tiny one-off jobs where a $15 flat fee is a large % of a $40 task; anyone assuming “commission-free” means $0 total fees.

Conflicting figures (flagged)

Unverified / conflicting marketing copy. Some Contra marketing and older intro pages have mentioned different client fee figures (for example a “$19 contract fee” or “starts as low as $2”). Those conflict with the Fees overview table above ($15 / $29). Until Contra consolidates the numbers, we treat the Fees overview help article as the primary source and mark other figures unverified.

Upwork vs Contra: marketplace fees vs commission-free

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Different product shapes — not interchangeable

Short version. Upwork is an open marketplace with percentage client fees (Basic headline 5% / Business Plus 10% on the pricing page checked Nov 5, 2026) plus a freelancer-side service fee. Contra markets 0% commission for Independents; clients on the Free plan pay a flat platform fee of $15 (payments under $500) or $29 ($500+) per Contra’s Fees overview — and processing fees still apply.
Upwork 5% vs 7.99% conflict. Same caveat as our other Nov 5 comparisons: hero/examples show 5%/10% while some FAQ blocks still mention 7.99%. We cite 5%/10% and flag the conflict.

Fee shape comparison

What each side typically pays (sourced; processing fees extra on Contra)
ItemUpworkContra (Fees overview)
Client platform fee Basic 5% (3% eligible U.S. bank); Business Plus 10% (8% bank). Contract Initiation Fee on many new contracts. Free plan: $15 if payment < $500; $29 if $500+. Pro Independents can waive client platform fees (subscription required).
Freelancer / Independent take-rate 0%–15% Freelancer Service Fee per contract. 0% commission for Independents (Contra’s core claim). Payout-rail / processor fees still exist.
Marketplace style Open job posts, proposals, Project Catalog, escrow milestones, hourly tracker. Network + contracts/invoices oriented toward creatives; not a giant open bid marketplace in the Upwork sense.

Worked examples (platform fee only)

  • $400 payment: Contra Free client platform fee $15 (3.75% of $400). Upwork Basic at 5% ≈ $20 before initiation fee.
  • $2,000 payment: Contra Free client fee $29 (1.45%). Upwork Basic at 5% ≈ $100. Contra’s flat fee wins on larger invoices if both parties already want Contra’s workflow.
  • Freelancer take-home: On Contra, Independent keeps the $2,000 before processor/payout fees. On Upwork, subtract the locked Freelancer Service Fee %.

When Contra’s model fits vs Upwork

  • Contra fits when you already have (or can find) an Independent on Contra and want 0% creative commission with a predictable flat client fee.
  • Upwork fits when you need a large open supply of proposals, hourly tracking, or Upwork-specific protection tooling — and accept percentage client fees.
  • Neither replaces reading escrow/payment-protection rules before funding work.

FAQ

Does commission-free mean free?

No. Contra’s 0% Independent commission is not the same as $0 total cost. Free-plan clients pay $15 or $29 platform fees, and payment processors charge separately.

Which is cheaper on a $150 job?

On a $150 payment, Contra’s $15 flat fee is 10% of the payment — often higher than Upwork Basic’s 5% ($7.50) before initiation fees. Flat fees hurt more on tiny jobs.

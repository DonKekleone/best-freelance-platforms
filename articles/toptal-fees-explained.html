Fees · Toptal

Toptal fees explained for clients

Last checked: Nov 6, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official FAQ + Platform Subscription ToS · Not a rate card

Short version. Toptal is not a percentage marketplace. Clients pay a published $79/month platform subscription once they proceed with matching, plus whatever talent rate Toptal quotes for the engagement. There is no public rate card on the FAQ. Engagements start with a trial of up to two weeks that is unbilled if you are not satisfied.
Unverified elsewhere. Blog posts that list “typical” Toptal hourly bands, deposit amounts, or markup percentages are not treated as official here. Budget from a written Toptal quote only.

Subscription terms that change the $79 math

  • The Platform Subscription Terms say the monthly fee is the amount shown at sign-up (or later communicated), and that you need written notice at least three business days before the next billing date to cancel, provided you do not have an active engagement.
  • Subscription fees are described as non-refundable, except you may request a refund of up to one month if you cancel because Toptal is unable to present any talent.
  • Read the live ToS before budgeting; contract language controls over this summary.

What you cannot honestly compare as a single %

Upwork and Fiverr publish marketplace take-rates. Toptal’s client cost is mostly the quoted talent rate plus the $79 subscription. A $5,000 Upwork month at Basic 5% is about $250 in marketplace fee on top of freelancers’ rates. A Toptal month is $79 plus whatever quote you accepted — which may be higher or lower for the same skill, but you cannot reverse-engineer it from a blog “average.”

IP and billing hygiene (FAQ claims)

  • FAQ: work created by a Toptal expert is the client’s via Toptal’s pass-through IP arrangement (confirm in your signed STA / agreements).
  • FAQ: if an expert is unavailable for scheduled time, Toptal credits back the time billed (their claim — keep invoice notes).

FAQ

Does Toptal publish hourly rates?

No. The FAQ does not publish a client rate card. You receive a written quote for talent. Third-party “average Toptal rate” blogs are not official and should not be budgeted as facts.

What is the $79 charge?

Toptal’s FAQ lists a flat $79 monthly platform subscription if you decide to proceed with talent matching. It gives access to the client platform under the Platform Subscription Terms. You may cancel; if Toptal cannot provide talent, you can request a refund on cancellation.

What happens during the trial?

Engagements start with a trial of up to two weeks. If you are completely satisfied, Toptal bills for that time and continues. If not, you are not billed for that trial period, and Toptal may introduce another expert for a second no-risk trial. Clients may trial up to three individuals per position.

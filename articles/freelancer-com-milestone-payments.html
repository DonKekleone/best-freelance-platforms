Freelancer.com Milestone Payment System explained

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Primary source: Freelancer.com Fees and Charges

Short version. Freelancer.com’s Milestone Payment System holds employer funds until release, refund, or dispute handling under the User Agreement. Arbitration of a milestone dispute costs $5 or 5% (whichever greater), refunded to the winner.

Funding and release

  • For Services, employers fund the service price; release only when the employer is satisfied (fees page language).
  • For projects, milestone deposits sit until released, refunded, or handled under dispute rules.
  • Employers can cancel an awarded project within seven days after acceptance for a full refund of the employer project fee (separate from milestone balances — re-read the live fees page for interaction details).

Arbitration window

  • After 4 days of a dispute (or 14 days if the freelancer filed), either party may move to paid arbitration.
  • The other party has 4 days to pay the fee and submit evidence or loses.
  • Fee: $5 or 5% of the disputed milestone, refunded to the winner.

Related fees that interact with milestones

  • Employer project fee 3%/$3; freelancer 10%/$5 — see Freelancer.com fees.
  • Offsiting penalty up to 30% of max budget if terms are violated — off-platform rules.
  • Broader escrow context: escrow & milestones.

FAQ

Who pays Freelancer.com arbitration?

The party that elects paid arbitration pays $5 or 5% of the disputed milestone (greater). The fee is refunded to the winner.

Is a milestone the same as escrow?

Functionally similar: funds are committed on-platform before release. Exact legal framing is Freelancer.com’s Milestone Payment System under its User Agreement.

Cornerstone · Fees

Freelance platform fee comparison: Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer.com, Toptal, PeoplePerHour

Last checked: Nov 6, 2026 (America/Denver) · Sources linked below · Outbound links go to official platform pages

Upwork fee conflict (flagged). Upwork’s client pricing page (checked Nov 5, 2026) leads with Basic 5% (or 3% for eligible U.S. checking-account payers) and Business Plus 10% (or 8% with the bank discount), plus Contract Initiation Fees. Some FAQ/schema blocks on the same page still mention 7.99%. We treat the hero/examples 5% / 10% as the current public headline and mark 7.99% as conflicting; your checkout is authoritative.
Read this first. Platform fees change. Memberships, payment method, geography, contract type, FX, taxes, withdrawal fees, and optional upgrades can all move the real total. The table below is a starting map, not a quote. Always re-check the official fee page before you budget a hire.

99designs 1-to-1 Projects: fixed 5% client platform fee on each project. Platform fee by level: 15% Entry / 10% Mid / 5% Top, plus a new-client introduction fee of 20% of the first $500 (max $100) unless waived. Deep dive: 99designs fees explained.
Malt Plan % on freelancer invoice excl. tax: headline Starter 10% / Advanced 15% / Enterprise custom; regional tables differ (e.g. Belgium 5%/9%). PeoplePerHour Buyer / payment-processing fees are omitted here until we confirm them from an official page. Service fee on earnings by lifetime billing per buyer: 20% below £250; 7.5% from £250–£5,000; 3.5% above £5,000 (excl. VAT). Min service fee £1/invoice. Bands are lifetime per buyer relationship, not global lifetime earnings.

How to think about “total cost”

  1. Separate client fees from freelancer fees. A 20% seller commission is not the same as a 5.5% buyer service fee — both can apply on the same order.
  2. Watch fixed add-ons on small jobs. Fiverr’s $3.50 under-$200 fee hits short logos harder than a $2,000 build.
  3. Membership and connects are not free. Upwork Connects and Freelancer memberships/upgrades change effective cost before a contract exists. See Upwork Connects cost explained and Freelancer.com membership plans. Fiverr Pro buyer plans: Fiverr Pro pricing for buyers.
  4. Withdrawal, FX, and tax can exceed the headline % on cross-border work.
  5. Screened networks price differently. Toptal’s cost is mostly the talent rate you are quoted, not a transparent marketplace take-rate.

Worked examples (illustrative only)

Assume a $500 fixed-scope design job, no upgrades, no FX, no tax. These are arithmetic illustrations from the sourced headline rates — not invoices.

  • Fiverr: Client pays ~$500 + 5.5% + $3.50 ≈ $531. Freelancer receives 80% of $500 = $400 before withdrawal/clearing.
  • Freelancer.com: Client project fee max(3% of $500, $3) = $15. Freelancer fee max(10% of $500, $5) = $50.
  • Upwork: Depends on plan and the freelancer’s locked service-fee %. Example only: Basic client at 5% pays ~$25 on $500; a freelancer at 10% keeps $450 of $500 billed. Re-check the offer screen for the live %.
  • PeoplePerHour: On a first £500 relationship that crosses the £250 band mid-engagement, blended freelancer fees apply per the band rules — do not assume a flat 20%.
  • Toptal: $79 subscription + quoted talent rate. No honest single-% comparison without a quote.
  • Workana: Client ~$22.50 service cost on $500 (4.5%); freelancer at opening 20% band keeps $400 before withdrawal.
  • 99designs: First 1-to-1 project with a new client, Entry Level designer: client pays ~$25 (5%); designer pays $75 platform fee + $100 introduction fee and keeps ~$325 (use the quote calculator for the live figure).
  • Malt: Depends on client plan country % plus the on-screen freelancer fee — do not flatten to one marketplace %.

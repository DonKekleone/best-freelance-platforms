Paying freelancers off-platform: Upwork, Fiverr and Freelancer.com rules

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Primary sources: each platform’s terms and help center

Why this matters. “Let’s skip the fees, pay me directly” is the most common way people lose both their money and their account. All three marketplaces prohibit it in their terms, and all three say they cannot help once money moves outside.

Worked example: Upwork conversion

Using the Help Center formula, a $40/hour freelancer: 13.5% × $40 × 2,080 = $11,232. A $3/hour rate would compute to $842, but the User Agreement’s $1,000 minimum would apply. The fee is paid in full with one payment method; the parties decide who pays.

What you give up off-platform

  • Escrow or hourly protection (Upwork, Fiverr).
  • Dispute resolution and support.
  • Public feedback and visible work history.

If someone asks you to move off-platform

  • Decline in writing on the platform and keep the record.
  • Report it through the platform’s reporting tool.
  • If a long-term direct relationship makes sense, use the documented route (Upwork Conversion Fee) instead of a side deal.
  • For relationships that began outside any marketplace, set up your own written contract and payment safeguards: reducing non-payment risk.

FAQ

Can I pay an Upwork freelancer outside Upwork?

Only after paying Upwork's Conversion Fee for that relationship, or after the 24-month non-circumvention period. Upwork's Help Center gives the fee as 13.5% of the hourly rate times 2,080 hours; the User Agreement sets a $1,000 minimum and $50,000 maximum.

Does Fiverr allow paying outside Fiverr?

No. Fiverr's off-platform policy prohibits offering or accepting payments outside Fiverr, and violations can lead to suspension.

What is Freelancer.com's offsiting fee?

30% of the maximum project budget or the agreed amount, per the Fees and Charges page checked Nov 5, 2026.

Workana fees explained

Last checked: Nov 6, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official Help + Max pricing page

Short version. Clients pay a 4.5% service cost on contracted payments ($2 minimum) plus dynamic payment-processing fees. Freelancers pay a relationship-tier commission of 20% → 10% → 5% as the same client pays more over time (USD bands). Optional Workana Max drops the client commission to 0%; freelancers still pay theirs.

How the freelancer tiers work

  • Bands are calculated in USD on the total the same client has paid that freelancer, regardless of either party’s country.
  • Help says all payments count: fixed-price, hourly, extensions, direct hire, and direct payments.
  • Once cumulative paid value for that client exceeds $300, the freelancer’s commission on further work with them is 10%; above $3000 it is 5%.
  • Enterprise client projects may have commission subject to review (Help note).
  • Help warns freelancers not to discuss Workana’s commission with the client as a disintermediation invite (sanction risk).

$500 worked example (illustrative)

Assume a first hire with a new client, no Max plan, ignore processing/FX/tax:

  • Client service cost: 4.5% of $500 = $22.50 (above the $2 floor).
  • Freelancer at the opening 20% band: keeps $400 of $500 before withdrawal.
  • If that same client later takes the relationship past $3000 paid, later payments to that freelancer are at the 5% freelancer band.

FAQ

What does a Workana client pay?

On a contract, the client pays a service cost of 4.5% of the total, with a minimum of $2 USD. Payment-processing fees are separate and shown at checkout. Posting a project and messaging freelancers is free. Workana Max clients pay 0% client commission.

What commission does a Workana freelancer pay?

Freelancer commission is tiered by lifetime USD paid by that same client: 20% from $0–$300, 10% from $301–$3000, and 5% above $3000. All payment types with that client count. Enterprise projects may be reviewed separately.

What is Workana Max?

Workana Max is an optional client subscription listed at $299 USD/year or $199 USD every 6 months. Max clients pay 0% client commission; freelancers still pay their relationship-tier commission.

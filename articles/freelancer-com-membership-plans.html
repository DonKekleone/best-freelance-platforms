Freelancer.com membership plans compared

Last checked: Nov 7, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official membership page + Fees and Charges + Preferred Freelancer Program

Short version. Free accounts can bid with a small monthly bid pool. Paid plans buy more bids and extras: Basic $4.99, Plus $9.99 (1-month trial), Professional $49, Premier $99 per month (USD on the public page). Project commission is still set on the Fees and Charges page, not replaced by these subscriptions.

What membership buys (and what it does not)

  • Paid plans mainly raise bid caps, skill slots, contest entry perks, employer followings, and some upgrade/invoice extras listed on the membership page.
  • Core employer/freelancer project fees remain on Freelancer.com fees explained (3% employer / 10% or $5 freelancer on regular fixed projects, with other situational fees).
  • The Fees and Charges FAQ mentions paid plans can include “reduced fees” among benefits, but the public membership cards do not publish a clear per-plan commission table. Treat any fee discount as unverified until your account’s checkout shows it.
Flagged / unverified. We do not invent a membership discount on the 10% project fee. Re-check Fees and Charges and your plan benefits screen before bidding as if commission is lower.

Preferred Freelancer Program vs membership

  • Program entry itself is free, but the Preferred Freelancer SLA says you must maintain a paid membership and Verified by Freelancer status (plus review, completion, and other performance rules).
  • On Recruiter projects only, Preferred freelancers pay 15% when payment is received instead of the usual upfront project-fee timing. Regular projects keep the normal fee structure.

When a paid plan can make sense

  • Fits: You burn through free bids quickly, need daily withdrawals, or want Preferred eligibility and higher bid volume on a steady pipeline.
  • Awkward: Occasional bidding where $49–$99/month dwarfs expected awards, or employers who only post (posting a project is free; you pay at award).

FAQ

How much do Freelancer.com membership plans cost?

Published monthly USD prices are Basic $4.99, Plus $9.99 (with a one-month free trial), Professional $49.00, and Premier $99.00. Annual billing lists Basic at $54.00/year, Professional at $478.80/year, and Premier at $958.80/year. Currency and taxes can vary by country.

How many bids do free Freelancer.com members get?

The Fees and Charges page states free members get 6 bids per month, and a minimum account balance (often $20) is required to bid.

Does Preferred Freelancer change membership fees?

Joining the Preferred Freelancer Program is free, but the service-level agreement requires a paid membership and Verified by Freelancer status among other criteria. On Recruiter projects only, Preferred freelancers pay a 15% project fee when payment is received instead of the usual upfront fee structure.

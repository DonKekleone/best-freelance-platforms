Freelancer.com fees explained

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Primary source: Freelancer.com Fees and Charges page

Short version. Employers pay 3% or $3 (whichever is greater) on fixed-price awards and 3% on each hourly payment. Freelancers pay 10% or $5 (whichever is greater) on fixed-price awards and 10% on hourly payments. A long list of optional and situational fees sits on top.

Employers can cancel an awarded project from the dashboard for up to seven days after acceptance for a full refund of the employer fee, per the same page.

Worked examples (illustrative, before payment-method fees and tax)

  • $50 fixed project: employer fee $3 (3% would be $1.50, so the $3 minimum applies); freelancer fee $5 (10% would be $5).
  • $500 fixed project: employer fee $15; freelancer fee $50.
  • $1,000 hourly over a month: employer pays 3% of each payment ($30 total); freelancer pays 10% ($100 total).

Fees freelancers often miss

  • Bid balance: free members start with 6 bids per month, and a minimum account balance of $20 must be held to bid; Freelancer.com may set it higher for risk-based or high-value bids.
  • Withdrawals: $1 for Express, PayPal, Skrill or Payoneer card; $25 for international wire; minimum withdrawal $50 after fees.
  • Inactive accounts: maintenance fee of up to $14/month after six months without login (refundable on request after reactivation, per the page).
  • Chargeback recovery: $25 or 10% of the milestone, whichever is higher, when the freelancer is found at fault.
  • Verified by Freelancer badge: $99 regular application (non-refundable), $79 renewal.

Fees employers often miss

  • Payment method: $0.30 + 2.3% for card, PayPal or Skrill (non-Australian transactions); local bank deposit free; international wire $15.
  • Optional project upgrades: e.g. Featured, Urgent, Sealed $10.99; Private, NDA, IP Agreement $22.99; Full Time $240 (fee table).
  • Australian transactions: Account Admin Fee AUD $0.75 + 0.99% (discounts possible at Freelancer’s discretion).
Conflicting figures (flagged). On the same page, the FAQ lists upgrades at $9.99 / $21.99 / $229.99, while the fee table lists $10.99 / $22.99 / $240. We report the table values and treat the FAQ figures as possibly outdated. Your checkout is the final word.

Disputes and going off-platform

  • Milestone arbitration: $5 or 5%, whichever is greater. Either party may escalate after 4 days (14 days if the freelancer filed); the other side has 4 days to pay or loses the dispute; the fee is refunded to the winner.
  • Offsiting penalty: users who communicate and transact outside the platform in violation of the terms may be charged 30% of the maximum project budget or agreed amount. See our off-platform payment rules guide.

How it compares

Freelancer.com’s 3% employer fee is lower than Fiverr’s 5.5% buyer fee, but its freelancer fee floor and paid-bid mechanics differ from Upwork’s per-contract Freelancer Service Fee. See the multi-platform fee comparison and hourly vs fixed-price contracts.

FAQ

What does Freelancer.com charge employers?

3% of the project value or $3.00 USD, whichever is greater, on fixed-price projects (charged at award), and 3% of each payment on hourly projects, per the Fees and Charges page checked Nov 5, 2026. Payment-method fees and optional upgrades are extra.

What does Freelancer.com charge freelancers?

10% or $5.00 USD, whichever is greater, on fixed-price projects and 10% of each hourly payment. Preferred Freelancer Program members pay 15% on Recruiter projects. A $20 minimum account balance is required to bid.

How much is Freelancer.com arbitration?

$5.00 USD or 5% of the disputed milestone, whichever is greater, refunded to the winner.

PeoplePerHour fees explained

Last checked: Nov 7, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official PeoplePerHour Help + Terms (modified Oct 28, 2026)

Short version. Freelancers pay a service fee by lifetime billing with each buyer: 20% below £250 (or $350 / €300), 7.5% up to £5,000 (or $7,000 / €6,000), and 3.5% above that, all excl. VAT. Most buyers pay £0.60 + 10%. Bank transfer is listed at 0% for Gold members and 2.5% for Silver members (Premium Programme).
Clearance wording conflict (flagged). PeoplePerHour Help on withdrawing money (updated May 31, 2024) says funds become available after a clearing period of about 14 working days. The Terms (last modified Oct 28, 2026) instead say 14 days after the buyer approves the invoice, shortened to 7 days when “Recent Income” over the last two calendar months is between £2,000 and £6,000, or 3 days when Recent Income is more than £6,000. We do not pick a winner; the account withdrawal screen is authoritative.

How the freelancer bands work

The fee resets with each buyer. A new client starts in the 20% band even if you already earn a lot from other clients. Help also says there is no registration fee, no fee to search for projects, and no fee to post an offer.

PeoplePerHour describes a Zero Service Fee scheme for work with existing off-platform clients whom you invite to the site under the Terms’ eligibility rules (the work was not originally sourced on PeoplePerHour, the client must be new to the platform, and they must pay at least one invoice there). Invited clients who sign up receive a £30 coupon on a first purchase of £50 or more, with a monthly cap on coupons redeemed through one freelancer’s invitations.

£500 first-project example (illustrative)

Assume a brand-new buyer relationship, a single £500 invoice, no VAT on the service fee, no FX and no withdrawal yet. The published bands put the first portion below £250 at 20% and the rest at 7.5%.

  • Freelancer service fee: £250 × 20% = £50, plus £250 × 7.5% = £18.75, total £68.75. The freelancer keeps £431.25 before withdrawal fees.
  • Buyer card or PayPal fee, if the listed 10% applies to that £500 invoice: £0.60 + £50 = £50.60.
  • A later £500 invoice with the same buyer, once lifetime billing with that buyer is already above £250 and still under £5,000, would be 7.5% = £37.50 to PeoplePerHour.

FAQ

What commission does a PeoplePerHour freelancer pay?

PeoplePerHour charges a service fee on freelancer earnings by lifetime billing with each buyer: 20% below £250 (or $350 / €300), 7.5% from that threshold up to £5,000 (or $7,000 / €6,000), and 3.5% above that (all excl. VAT). The minimum service fee per invoice is £1 / $1.40 / €1.20 (excl. VAT).

What does a PeoplePerHour buyer pay?

The Terms list buyer service fees of £0.60 plus 10% for PayPal, cards and several other methods. Bank transfer is listed at 0% for Gold members and 2.5% for Silver members, available to Premium Programme members as of 3 April 2019. Optional extras include an urgent listing fee of £15 (or €20 / $20), an NDA fee of £35 (or €40 / $40), and a featured project fee of £20 (or €25 / $25).

When can a PeoplePerHour freelancer withdraw earnings?

The Terms (modified 28 October 2026) say funds become available 14 days after the buyer approves the invoice, or 7 days if recent income over the last two calendar months is between £2,000 and £6,000, or 3 days if recent income is more than £6,000. A Help article updated 31 May 2024 instead describes the clearing period as approximately 14 working days. Withdrawal method fees are listed separately in the Terms.

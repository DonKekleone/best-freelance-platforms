PayPal Goods and Services vs Friends and Family for freelancers

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · US merchant fees page

Marketplace warning. Moving an Upwork/Fiverr job to PayPal can void platform protection and may violate off-platform rules. See off-platform payment rules.
Short version. For paid client work, use Goods and Services (or commercial invoice/checkout paths). Friends and Family is for personal transfers — lower or no receive fee in many domestic cases, but the wrong tool when a client later disputes a delivery.

Fee anchors (US business, checked Nov 5, 2026)

  • Send/Receive Money for Goods and Services: 2.99% (domestic commercial table on PayPal’s merchant fees page).
  • PayPal Checkout / many commercial types: 3.49% + $0.49 USD fixed (currency table).
  • International commercial receive: domestic rate + 1.50%.

Operational differences that matter

  • Goods and Services / commercial: seller fees apply; eligible purchase-protection / dispute paths exist for qualifying goods/services transactions.
  • Friends and Family: typically intended for personal payments; using it for client work can leave you exposed if the payment is reversed or the relationship sours — and some clients ask for it specifically to avoid seller fees.
  • Invoices: invoicing rates on the same fees page often mirror Checkout or card rates depending on how the buyer pays.

Compare bank-detail rails: Wise vs PayPal.

FAQ

Should freelancers accept Friends and Family from clients?

We advise against it for paid client work. Goods and Services (or a commercial invoice) is the appropriate path.

Is 2.99% always what I pay?

No. Checkout, cards, international, and currency conversion can change the total. Read the live PayPal merchant fees page for your payment type.

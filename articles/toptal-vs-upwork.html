Toptal vs Upwork: screened network vs open marketplace

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official FAQ / Help pages · Not a rate card

These products solve different jobs. Upwork is an open marketplace where you post work and compare proposals (or buy Project Catalog). Toptal is a curated matching network with a subscription, quoted rates, and a trial period. Comparing only “hourly rate blogs” is how budgets get lied to.
Upwork fee conflict (flagged). Upwork’s client pricing page (checked Nov 5, 2026) leads with Basic 5% (or 3% for eligible U.S. checking-account payers) and Business Plus 10% (or 8% with the bank discount), plus Contract Initiation Fees. Some FAQ/schema blocks on the same page still mention 7.99%. We treat the hero/examples 5% / 10% as the current public headline and mark 7.99% as conflicting; your checkout is authoritative. Re-checked Nov 9, 2026: the conflict is still live — the pricing page still shows 5%/10% in its plan cards and examples, while Upwork Help’s Client Marketplace Fee article still says Basic clients pay “up to 7.99%” (3% with the eligible U.S. bank discount). Treat 5% as the published headline and 7.99% as a possible ceiling until Upwork reconciles the two.

At a glance

Structural differences (sourced where noted)
DimensionUpworkToptal
How you find talent Post a job, invite freelancers, or buy Project Catalog offerings. You screen proposals yourself. Request matching; Toptal introduces candidates from its network. FAQ claims introductions often within 24 hours and that fewer than 3% of applicants are accepted (Toptal’s own screening claim — not independently audited here).
Client platform cost Basic: Client Marketplace Fee 5% (pricing-page headline) (eligible U.S. bank 3%). Business Plus: 10% (eligible checking 8%) + possible Contract Initiation Fee up to $4.99 under $100. See Upwork fees. FAQ: flat $79/month subscription if you proceed with matching; cancel anytime; refund request if Toptal cannot provide talent.
Talent price transparency Freelancer rates are visible on profiles/proposals; Freelancer Service Fee 0–15% is locked per contract. No public rate card on the FAQ. You get a written quote. Do not treat third-party “average Toptal rate” articles as official.
Trial / risk buffer Fixed-price: fund milestones before work. Hourly: tracked time + Hourly Protection rules. See escrow guide. FAQ: trial of up to two weeks; if not completely satisfied, you are not billed for that trial period; up to three individuals per position for trials.
Best-fit job shape Variable scope, many candidates to compare, budget-sensitive one-offs, ongoing marketplace relationships. Senior specialized talent, speed of matching over browsing, willingness to pay network premiums for screening.

Payment and IP notes

  • Upwork: Milestone funding and dispute flows are platform-defined. Client and freelancer fees both apply — see fee guides.
  • Toptal: FAQ states invoices are typically twice a month with Net 10 terms; payment methods include major cards, ACH, wires, PayPal. FAQ states work product is the client’s via Toptal’s pass-through IP arrangement (confirm in your signed agreements).

Decision checklist

  • Is the work a small packaged task under a few hundred dollars? Marketplace (or Fiverr Gig) usually fits better than a $79 subscription network.
  • Do you need many competing proposals to discover price? Upwork.
  • Do you want matching + trial before commitment, and can you afford quoted senior rates? Ask Toptal for a quote.
  • Have you written acceptance criteria? Neither platform fixes a vague brief — use a hiring brief.

What we are not claiming

  • No claim that Toptal is “top 3% of the world’s freelancers” as an independent fact — that is Toptal’s screening statistic about its applicant pool.
  • No invented average hourly rates for either network.

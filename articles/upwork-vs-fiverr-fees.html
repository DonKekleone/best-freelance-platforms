Upwork vs Fiverr fees for clients

Last checked: Nov 5, 2026 (America/Denver) · Client-side only · Freelancer take-rates covered in sibling guides

Upwork fee conflict (flagged). Upwork’s client pricing page (checked Nov 5, 2026) leads with Basic 5% (or 3% for eligible U.S. checking-account payers) and Business Plus 10% (or 8% with the bank discount), plus Contract Initiation Fees. Some FAQ/schema blocks on the same page still mention 7.99%. We treat the hero/examples 5% / 10% as the current public headline and mark 7.99% as conflicting; your checkout is authoritative.
Scope. This page compares what clients pay in marketplace/service fees. Freelancer commissions (Fiverr ~20% / Upwork 0–15%) still shape the sticker price freelancers quote you — they are not free. Full breakdowns: Upwork fees, Fiverr fees.

Takeaways from the math (not a ranking):

  • On small under-$200 Fiverr orders, the $3.50 add-on can make Fiverr’s client fee higher than Upwork Basic’s 5% percentage alone.
  • On mid and large prepaid Gigs, Fiverr’s 5.5% is often lower than Upwork Basic’s 5%.
  • Eligible Upwork U.S. bank payers at 3% can undercut both headline percentages — if that billing method is available to you.
  • Business Plus’s 10%/8% is a different product (team features, Net 30 option) — do not compare it only on fee %.

Fee shape is not the whole decision

  1. How you buy. Fiverr is typically prepaid Gig/order; Upwork often uses funded milestones or hourly. See escrow and milestones.
  2. Who sets scope. Packaged Gigs vs open job posts change revision risk more than a 2% fee gap.
  3. Freelancer net. A Fiverr seller keeping 80% may price higher than an Upwork seller on a low service-fee contract. Compare all-in quotes, not fee % in isolation.

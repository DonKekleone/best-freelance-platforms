Upwork Connects cost explained

Last checked: Nov 6, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official Help + freelancer pricing · No affiliate links

Short version. Connects are Upwork’s virtual tokens for proposals and some promo tools. Official Help price: $0.15 USD each. Freelancer Plus is published at $19.99/month with 100 Connects/month. Basic does not list a free automatic allotment on the pricing page — it says earn up to 10 by completing tasks.
Check in-product. Proposal Connect costs can change while a job is live. Freelancer Plus billed through the Apple App Store can use different pricing than the web pricing page. Agency Connects follow separate rules and balances.

What Connects are used for

  • Submit a proposal — the job post shows the base Connects required (can change while the job is live).
  • Boost a proposal — optional extra Connects for a promoted position.
  • Boost your profile / Boosted Messages / Availability Badge — bid/budget models; Connects charged for eligible interactions or while the badge is on.
  • Identity verification badge — optional; 35 Connects on Basic per Help/pricing.

Using Connects does not guarantee a view, interview, or hire.

When Connects are not required

  • Responding to a client invitation to apply (accept or decline).
  • Accepting a direct offer.
  • Invitation-only Talent Accelerator opportunities.
  • Working on or messaging about an existing contract.

Help notes some new freelancers must first make a qualifying Connects purchase or join Freelancer Plus before submitting marketplace proposals, including invitation responses.

Rough math (illustrative only)

  • 100 purchased Connects ≈ $15 at $0.15 each.
  • Freelancer Plus at $19.99 with 100 included Connects is roughly the same Connects face value as buying 100, plus the other Plus features on the pricing page (job alerts, proposal insights, 0% Direct Contracts fee for your own clients, profile tools).
  • If a proposal costs 16 Connects, that is about $2.40 in purchased Connects — before any boost.

Agency note

Freelancer and agency Connects balances are separate. Agency Basic cannot buy agency Connects or pursue most new non-Enterprise work; Agency Plus is required for other new agency work. Agency unused Connects may roll over up to a monthly cap (Help: up to 400). Do not treat freelancer rules as agency rules.

FAQ

How much does one Connect cost?

Upwork Help states Connects cost $0.15 USD each. They are sold in bundles, and a custom amount may also be available on the purchase page. Buying Connects is not available in the Upwork mobile app.

Does Freelancer Basic include free Connects every month?

Upwork’s freelancer pricing page lists Basic monthly Connects as 0, with a note that you can earn up to 10 Connects monthly by completing tasks that help you find work. Do not budget as if 10 Connects are automatic.

What does Freelancer Plus include for Connects?

The freelancer pricing page lists Freelancer Plus at $19.99 per month with 100 Connects each month. Help says first-month Connects may be 90–100 depending on when you upgrade. Apple App Store subscriptions can use different pricing — check your Membership plan screen.

Do unused Connects expire?

Yes. Help says every Connect expires one year after the date it was issued. Unused freelancer Connects roll over with no monthly freelancer rollover limit. Expired Connects cannot be restored or refunded. Upwork does not email reminders before expiration.

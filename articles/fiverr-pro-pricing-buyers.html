Fiverr Pro pricing for buyers

Last checked: Nov 7, 2026 (America/Denver) · Official Fiverr Pro pricing page + Help Center

Short version. Essential has no subscription charge if you stay eligible by ordering $1,000+ of services a year. Advanced is $129/month. Neither plan replaces the regular buyer service fee on each order (5.5%, plus $3.50 under $200).

Subscription vs order fees

  • The Pro plans page says Essential will not auto-charge a payment method. If you do not hit the $1,000 annual order threshold, Fiverr lists Advanced at $129/month or switching back to standard Fiverr as the options.
  • You cannot buy Essential with a flat subscription, and you cannot pay Advanced with Fiverr balance, credits, or coupons.
  • Only admins can upgrade to Advanced. Canceling Advanced returns a prorated refund for unused time; Essential features can continue at no cost.
  • Pro Help on payments still quotes the standard buyer service fee: 5.5% of the purchase amount, plus $3.50 when that payment is under $200. Extras and tips each get their own fee.
Flagged. Deferred or monthly payments on Advanced are eligibility-based. Do not assume every Advanced account gets them. Checkout and the Account plan page are the final word.

$500 order example (illustrative)

Assume one $500 Gig payment, no extras, no tip, no tax or FX.

  • Buyer service fee: 5.5% of $500 = $27.50 (no $3.50 add-on because the payment is not under $200).
  • Client pays about $527.50 for that order whether on Essential or Advanced.
  • Advanced adds $129/month on top only if you subscribe to that plan.

FAQ

How much does Fiverr Pro Essential cost?

Fiverr Pro Essential has no upfront subscription charge. After the first year, you keep Essential access by ordering services worth $1,000 or more in that year. Fiverr says it will not auto-charge a card for Essential if you miss that threshold.

How much is Fiverr Pro Advanced?

Fiverr Pro Advanced is $129 per month. Only account admins can upgrade. Fiverr balance, credits, and coupons cannot pay for the plan. Canceling yields a prorated refund for unused time, and Essential features can continue at no cost.

Do Fiverr Pro buyers still pay the regular service fee on orders?

Yes. Help for Pro billing still describes the standard buyer service fee as 5.5% of the purchase amount, plus a $3.50 fee on orders under $200, with a separate fee on each payment such as extras or tips.
